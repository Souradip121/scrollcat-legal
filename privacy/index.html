Scroll Cat Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Scroll Cat ("the app") is made by Souradip Pal ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app accesses, what it stores and what leaves your phone.

In short: your Reel counts, settings and camera never leave your phone, and we do not sell or share your data.

What the app accesses and why

  • Accessibility Service. With your permission, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect when the short-video screen of Instagram, YouTube or Facebook is open, count the videos you scroll, show a small counter on top, and leave the short-video screen when you reach your daily limit. It looks only for signs of those short-video screens. It does not read your messages, what you type, or anything in other apps. To tell one video from the next, it keeps a short fingerprint of a video's caption in memory while you watch; this is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Camera. The camera is used only during a workout, to count your reps. Each frame is processed on your phone and then discarded. No photo or video is recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Notifications. The app may send a notification when you reach your daily limit.
  • Internet. The app uses the internet to download the open-source motion tracking files it needs for workouts (Google MediaPipe, served from Google's and jsDelivr's servers) and to check your subscription. Like any download, these servers see your device's IP address. No camera data is sent.

What is stored, and where

Your daily Reel counts, your limit, your chosen apps and workouts, your cat's name and your streak are stored only on your phone. We have no account system and no server that receives this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Subscriptions and payments

Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and your locale and currency, so the app can tell whether you are subscribed. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data.

Children

Scroll Cat is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Your choices

You can turn off the Accessibility Service, camera or notifications at any time in your phone's Settings. You can cancel your subscription in Google Play. To ask us to delete the subscription data held by RevenueCat, email us at the address below.

Security

Data sent to RevenueCat and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.

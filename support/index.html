My apps are blocked. How do I get more time?

That's Scroll Cat working: you reached today's limit. Open Scroll Cat and feed your cat with a short workout to unlock a few more minutes (Android: more Reels). You can do up to 5 meals a day. The limit starts fresh every morning.

On iPhone and iPad, the "Feed" button on the block screen sends a notification; tap it to open the workout. If nothing appears, allow notifications for Scroll Cat in Settings > Notifications.

My apps stay blocked after a workout

  • Open Scroll Cat once after the workout so it can update your limit.
  • Check that your subscription or free trial is still active (Scroll Cat stops blocking anything when it isn't).
  • Still stuck? Deleting Scroll Cat removes all of its limits and blocks at once. Then email us so we can fix it.

How does Scroll Cat measure my scrolling?

  • Android: it counts the short videos you scroll in Instagram, YouTube and Facebook, using Android's Accessibility Service.
  • iPhone and iPad: Apple's Screen Time measures the minutes you spend in the apps you picked. An iPhone can't tell Reels apart from the rest of an app, so all the time in those apps counts. The meter moves in 2-minute steps.

Either way, what you watch and type never leaves your device. See the privacy policy.

The camera isn't counting my reps

  • Stand where the front camera can see the whole move, about 1.5 to 2 metres away.
  • Turn on a light or face a window; the app pauses when it's too dark.
  • Tap "Can't do this now?" for another move, or an easier one. Breathing moves need no camera.

Changing my limit or my apps

On the home screen, tap the line under the meter to change which apps are counted. On iPhone and iPad this opens Apple's app picker.

Subscriptions

  • Cancel: iPhone and iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > your profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.
  • New phone or reinstalled? On the subscription screen, tap "Restore purchases".
  • Refunds are handled by the store you paid: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through Google Play's order history.
